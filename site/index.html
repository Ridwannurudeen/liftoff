A token launch where being first is worthless.

Sealed Launch is a Uniswap v4 hook that launches a token through a sealed, uniform-price batch auction. Swaps are blocked during the window; everyone clears at one price, pro-rata. Snipers can't pay their way to the front, because there is no front.

Order in, order out — it doesn't matter.

01

Swaps blocked

During the launch window the hook reverts every swap. Nobody can trade the token before it clears — there's nothing to snipe.

02

Everyone commits

Buyers deposit quote tokens as commitments. Your block, your position, your gas — none of it changes your outcome.

03

One clearing price

At window close the auction settles at a single uniform price. Allocation = offered × yourCommit / total. The first-block buyer and the last-block buyer pay the same.

04

Pool opens

Settlement initializes the v4 pool at the clearing price, seeds liquidity, and opens trading. A missed minimum raise refunds everyone.

Why this is built for X Layer

In December 2025 X Layer moved to the OP Stack and runs a flashblocks sequencer. We tested real mainnet blocks: transactions are not ordered by priority fee — block ordering is effectively unpredictable. That breaks priority-fee "MEV-tax" hooks, and there's no general-purpose price oracle live on X Layer to power LVR-aware designs. So Sealed Launch doesn't fight ordering — it makes ordering irrelevant. A uniform-price batch auction is fair by construction. On a flashblock chain, it's the only launch that's provably un-snipeable — and it's directly adoptable by launchpads like flap.sh, which has no anti-snipe today.
